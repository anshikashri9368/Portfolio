Design that people don't have to think about

I'm a UI/UX Designer and Front-End Developer who enjoys turning complex ideas into simple, intuitive, and visually engaging digital experiences. I believe great design goes beyond how a product looks — it's about understanding people, solving real problems, and making every interaction feel effortless.

My process begins with understanding user needs and mapping out clear user flows, followed by wireframes, interactive prototypes, and high-fidelity interfaces in Figma. With my knowledge of HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and React, I can also bring those designs to life, bridging the gap between design and development.
