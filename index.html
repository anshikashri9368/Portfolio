Design that people don't have to think about.

I'm a UI/UX designer and front-end developer who turns complex ideas into simple, usable interfaces. Good design is less about how a product looks and more about understanding people and removing friction from what they are trying to do.

I start with user needs and clear flows, move through wireframes and interactive prototypes, and finish with high-fidelity screens in Figma. Because I also build with HTML, CSS, JavaScript and React, my designs are made with implementation in mind.
